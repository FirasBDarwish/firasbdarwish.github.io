For quite some time, I felt there was a missing link between Latent Action Models (LAMs) and World Models (WMs) that is highly relevant to the field of Robot Learning. As I gave this link some more thought, it helped me develop a philosophy on world modelling that I found was missing in today's world models.

For brevity's sake, I will begin my summarising one LAM paper and one WM paper. Then I will outline what the missing link, in my view, is, by drawing on these two works. Finally, I will propose a research direction for building better world models for robot control.
